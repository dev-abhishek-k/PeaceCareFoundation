---
name: Prototype copy convention
description: Project-specific wording preference for public-facing copy in the Peace Care Foundation preview.
---

Avoid the word “Sample” in user-facing content, and do not attribute the care story to a named individual or founder. Use “Preview” or “Illustrative” where those words accurately describe the content. Preserve truthful context about unverified activities and local-only forms, but do not restore notices the user has explicitly removed.
Until services are independently verified, use clearly labelled illustrative artwork for programme depictions. When the user directs use of their uploaded photos, show them as reference images only, with no implied affiliation or claim that they document the foundation's work. Permission was explicitly affirmed for an earlier group of photos; do not treat that as verification of pictured activities or as blanket permission for other uploads. When given a fundraising screenshot as a design reference, borrow its visual structure without copying unverified impact figures or implying that preview-only payment flows are live. If the user explicitly requests the full screenshot on the site, label it as a static design mockup and explain that embedded statistics and controls are not verified or functional. Distinguish any working controls placed near the screenshot from controls printed inside it.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested removing the named-person storyline and the “Sample” wording, and later asked to display supplied photos. The prototype still needs truthful boundaries so visitors do not mistake imagery for verified services. One supplied photo visibly features another organisation's branding. The user also preferred a less repetitive Donate page while retaining the form's preview-only messaging. They first chose a fundraising screenshot as a layout reference, then explicitly chose to show the full screenshot on the Home hero as a labelled reference. A later Home fixed-price image-card panel was explicitly removed; browsing care categories should not silently recreate it. Subsequent requests for more Home and Our Work categories did not ask to restore price choices. The user also explicitly removed the dark reference-photo overview panel from the Our Work hero; its photo and quick links should not be quietly reinstated. None of these choices verifies the screenshot's percentage or secure-payment claim.

**How to apply:** Check new page copy, form states, directory entries, navigation labels, page metadata and imagery before shipping. Keep reference photos separate from programme claims and label externally branded images plainly. Treat Home and Our Work care categories as illustrative exploration independent of Donate prices, unless the user explicitly requests otherwise. Do not restore the Our Work hero overview panel as filler.
