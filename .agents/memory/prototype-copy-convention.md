name: Prototype copy convention
description: Project-specific wording preference for public-facing copy in the Peace Care Foundation preview.
---

Avoid the word “Sample” in user-facing content, and do not attribute the care story to a named individual or founder. Use “Preview” or “Illustrative” where those words accurately describe the content. Preserve truthful context about unverified activities and local-only forms, but do not restore notices the user has explicitly removed.
Lead public-facing narrative with the real purpose and practical use cases of connecting people with independent NGOs, rather than framing every paragraph as a sample or prototype. Do not append “not confirmed programmes” or similar caveats to each cause description or card. Keep concise boundaries at fictional listings, local-only forms, payments and unverified imagery; do not turn examples into claims about active partners or services without verified details.
Do not restore the “Reference photo · Affiliation unverified” pill over the Browse NGOs, Request Help, Volunteer or Donate photos, or the reference-photo overlay on the Become a partner hero. Keep the surrounding copy truthful without adding another photo overlay; do not restore the separate caption below the Donate photo, the reassurance beneath its pledge button, or the explanatory paragraph beside its heading. On Volunteer, do not restore the removed left column beside the form (intro text, notice, reference photo and caption) or the small preview line beneath the hero button; keep the preview boundary in the form. Do not restore the separate example-listings/no-live-referrals line above Browse NGOs filters, the Home hero notice about example listings and non-live processing, or the secondary About hero paragraph about non-live referrals; profile cards still identify examples and forms retain their own boundaries. Do not restore the reference-photo captions beneath the About or Our Work hero photos, or “Planned · Not active” badges on About trust cards; the section introduction already clarifies their status.
Until services are independently verified, keep programme imagery contextualized without claiming it documents delivered work. Do not restore the “Illustrative artwork” overlay on Our Work category images; the user explicitly removed it. When the user directs use of their uploaded photos, show them as reference images only, with no implied affiliation or claim that they document the foundation's work. Permission was explicitly affirmed for an earlier group of photos; do not treat that as verification of pictured activities or as blanket permission for other uploads. When given a fundraising screenshot as a design reference, borrow its visual structure without copying unverified impact figures or implying that preview-only payment flows are live. If the user explicitly requests the full screenshot on the site, do not restore the explanatory caption or the design-reference header; the user removed both. The screenshot still contains an unverified percentage and printed pledge controls, so do not repeat them as factual claims elsewhere. Distinguish any working controls placed near the screenshot from controls printed inside it.

They also removed the large frontend-prototype notice from that hero and the generic verification paragraph from the category directory, while leaving more specific context within the category sections. On the Become a partner page, they explicitly removed the hero sentence explaining that enquiries cannot be received yet; keep the necessary preview boundary elsewhere without restoring that hero sentence.
