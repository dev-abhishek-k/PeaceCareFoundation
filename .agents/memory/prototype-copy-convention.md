---
name: Prototype copy convention
description: Project-specific wording preference for public-facing copy in the Peace Care Foundation preview.
---

Avoid the word “Sample” in user-facing content, and do not attribute the care story to a named individual or founder. Use “Preview” or “Illustrative” where those words accurately describe the content. Do not remove statements that the organisation or programmes are unverified, or imply that forms send requests or collect payments.
Until services are independently verified, use clearly labelled illustrative artwork for programme depictions. When the user directs use of their uploaded photos, show them as reference images only, with no implied affiliation or claim that they document the foundation's work. Permission was explicitly affirmed for an earlier group of photos; do not treat that as verification of pictured activities or as blanket permission for other uploads.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested removing the named-person storyline and the “Sample” wording, and later asked to display supplied photos. The prototype still needs truthful boundaries so visitors do not mistake imagery for verified services. One supplied photo visibly features another organisation's branding.

**How to apply:** Check new page copy, form states, directory entries, navigation labels, page metadata and imagery before shipping. Keep reference photos separate from programme claims and label externally branded images plainly.
