---
name: Prototype copy convention
description: Project-specific wording preference for public-facing copy in the Peace Care Foundation preview.
---

Avoid the word “Sample” in user-facing content, and do not attribute the care story to a named individual or founder. Use “Preview” or “Illustrative” where those words accurately describe the content. Do not remove statements that the organisation or programmes are unverified, or imply that forms send requests or collect payments.
Until services are independently verified, use clearly labelled illustrative artwork rather than visuals that appear to document actual activities.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested removing the named-person storyline and the “Sample” wording. The prototype still needs truthful boundaries so visitors do not mistake illustrative content or imagery for live services.

**How to apply:** Check new page copy, form states, directory entries, navigation labels, page metadata and imagery before shipping.
